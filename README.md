## About
**Tournamancy** is a <u>multiplayer</u> <u>first-person</u> <u>boomer shooter</u> where you play as a wizard honing your spellcrafting skills through friendly competition. Knock out your opponents to collect their spells and increase your repertoire of fantastical abilities. Get knocked out and come back ready for more until one wizard stands above the rest.

  * **Fast-Paced Gameplay** allows you to move, jump, and fly through Synth City to outmaneuver your opponents. Dodge projectiles and duck past melee attacks to close the distance.
  * **A Wide Variety of Weapons and Powerups** come in the form of "Spells," which you can loot from knocked-out opponents or from randomly-spawning prisms across the map.
  * **Free-for-all Multiplayer** means over N players can participate in the battle at a time.

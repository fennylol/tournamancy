# List of Spells
There are two types of spells: **Active Spells** and **Passive Spells**. A wizard can have up to two Active Spells, and activate their abilities by pressing a button on their mouse or controller. By contrast, a wizard can have any number of Passive Spells, each of which has a small, permanent effect that never goes away until you are knocked out.
